# Accepted differences and retained reference limitations

This file records individual owner decisions under [the reference-problem procedure](workplan.md#ask-the-owner-about-reference-problems) and [the rendering investigation limit](workplan.md#limit-unexplained-rendering-investigations). Approval of these procedures does not accept an individual behavior or visual difference.

## What each record includes

Use a stable ID such as AD01. Record the owner's decision date, affected case and check IDs, safe endpoint pattern, state and width, observed reference behavior, agreed outcome, reason, original and approved check definitions, required verification and private evidence label. Identify the decision as a retained reference limitation, an intentional Django behavior difference or an accepted visual difference.

A retained reference limitation means the owner chose to reproduce Rails, including its apparent problem; it is recorded here even when the applications match. An intentional Django behavior difference means the owner chose a specific expected outcome that differs from Rails. An accepted visual difference applies only to the precisely reviewed cases, states and widths. Keep exact bindings, personal information, screenshots and detailed evidence outside Git.

Record owner acceptance separately from implementation and verification. Preserve the old check definition and historical evidence when a replacement is approved. Complete a whole case only after its remaining required deployed checks pass or are explicitly covered by a recorded acceptance. Update [the case inventory](url_completion_inventory.md) and [progress.md](progress.md) only when whole-case evidence actually changes.
